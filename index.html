Bamboo Rampage: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Bamboo Rampage is a single-player game. The game itself collects no personal information. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some data to show and measure them, as set out below.

No accounts, no sign-in

The game never asks who you are.

Your progress stays on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, items and settings are saved only on your phone, in the game's private storage. The game sends none of it anywhere.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads between some levels and offers optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward (double coins, or an extra hero on a retry). To serve these, Google's AdMob SDK collects data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise them. See how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent before any ads load, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Settings, AD CHOICES. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings (Google, Ads, or Privacy, Ads).

Android backup

If you have Android's own backup turned on, Android may include the game's save in your device backup to your Google account, so your progress follows you to a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and Google under your Google account's settings.

No analytics or tracking by the game

Apart from the AdMob SDK described above, the game contains no analytics or tracking libraries.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the game itself collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if online leaderboards or analytics are added, this policy will be updated before the change ships.
